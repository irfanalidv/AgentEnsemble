## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 days of receiving the returned item. Opened software and gift cards cannot be refunded.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 USD.

## Returns
You can return any unused item within 30 days of delivery. Start a return from the Orders page and print the prepaid label.

## Password reset
To reset your password, click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link expires after 60 minutes.

## Warranty
Hardware products include a 2-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects but not accidental damage.
